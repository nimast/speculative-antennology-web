## Node 10 — "The Antenna Is Not a Metaphor"

### A. Full doc text (not currently in the exposition)

> The antenna should not be reduced to metaphor, although it inevitably attracts
> metaphorical thought. It is too exact for metaphor alone. An antenna receives,
> transmits, resonates, filters, converts, and distributes. Its form is shaped by
> wavelength, frequency, conductivity, impedance, directionality, gain, environment, and
> purpose. It belongs simultaneously to biology, engineering, communication, warfare,
> astronomy, domestic technology, ecology, and art. Biological antennae extend the body
> into chemical, tactile, and vibrational environments; engineered antennae extend
> technical systems into electromagnetic space. In both cases, sensing is not merely a
> mental or optical act but a morphological arrangement. To become sensitive, a body or
> device must take shape in relation to what it seeks to detect. This is why antennae
> matter philosophically. They materialise relation. They demonstrate that reception is
> never passive and that transmission is never pure departure. An antenna does not simply
> open itself to the world. It selects, shapes, privileges, excludes, and transforms. It
> is a form of attention made material. To think with antennae is therefore to think of
> art not as an object offered to perception, but as an apparatus configured for relation
> with forces that exceed unaided sense.

_Doc also carries an inline note here:_ **"(Antennae repository under or around this text nod?)"**
— a layout suggestion to place the archive/repository cluster near this node.

### B. Condensed versions currently live (Notion → site)

**`ess-6`**
> The antenna is the _only_ object explicitly designed to participate in that exchange —
> the shaped interface at which current becomes field, and field becomes current. Its
> geometry is not decoration. It is the partial embodiment of a theory of propagation. To
> catalogue antennas is to catalogue an infrastructure of assumption.

**`ess-9`**
> Antennae appeared first as biological appendages — extending the membrane of an animate
> body into its environment. Insect and crustacean antennae translate chemical,
> mechanical, and electromagnetic signals into electrical impulses a nervous system can
> read. Engineered antennae arrived later, doing the same work in metal: Maxwell's
> equations made into a rod. Form follows wavelength. The lengths and diameters of
> antennae are tuned to fractions of the waves they receive. The fan of a Rhipicera beetle
> maps the gradient of a pheromone; the parabola of a radio telescope focuses cosmic
> signal onto a receiver suspended at its focus. In biology and in engineering, the same
> constraint operates — proportion to the unseen.

— three islands in the same part of the canvas all arguing biological-vs-engineered antennae
and "form follows wavelength." The condensed pair keeps the technical specificity (current↔field,
Maxwell-as-rod, proportion to the unseen) without the longer philosophical register.

### What the full doc text adds that the condensed versions drop

- The explicit **"not a metaphor / too exact for metaphor"** thesis (the node's whole point).
- The catalogue of antenna operations (receives, transmits, resonates, filters, converts).
- **"reception is never passive… transmission is never pure departure"** — the philosophical payoff.
- **"a form of attention made material"** and the closing move to art-as-apparatus.

## Node 27 — "Energy Too Remembers"

### A. Full doc text (not currently in the exposition)

> Matter is often granted memory. It bears marks, scars, inscriptions, residues, ruins,
> sediments, and archives. Energy is more often imagined as passing: a flash, a pulse, a
> transmission, a charge, an event that leaves only secondary effects. Yet this division
> is philosophically insufficient. Energetic events alter systems. Radiation persists
> through decay. Electromagnetic pulses can leave residual differences. Fields can be
> modified. A wave can pass and still leave the world otherwise. To say that energy
> remembers is not to anthropomorphise energy, nor to claim that it remembers as minds or
> archives do. It is to insist that memory must be thought beyond the familiar forms of
> inscription on stable substrates. There are differences that continue, traces that are
> field-like rather than objectual, afterlives that cannot be reduced to documentation.
> This is the speculative horizon of the exposition: not the invisible as absence, not the
> imperceptible as lack, not energy as mere carrier, but energy as participant in the
> persistence of events. Such a horizon changes how artworks involving radiation,
> transmission, lightning, projection, simulation, and radio propagation may be understood.
> They do not simply pass through the world. They may leave it minutely, measurably,
> conceptually, or irreversibly altered.

### B. Condensed versions currently live (Notion → site)

**`ess-4`**
> The _material turn_ has urged us to treat matter as agentic and co-constitutive. But this
> discourse remains strikingly material-centric. Energy — radiation, electromagnetism, flux
> — has often been treated as evanescent, a carrier without remainder. This is misaligned
> with the electrodynamics we already use to navigate the world.

**`ess-5`**
> The _electromagnetic memory effect_ designates a permanent change induced by a finite
> radiation pulse — a residual kick to charged test bodies after the wave has passed. An
> energetic event that leaves a difference that continues. Not speculative: an entailment
> of the theory.

_(The pullquote `pq-1` — "Energy, too, remembers." — sits with this cluster.)_

### Why it was condensed

Logged in `REVISION_FLAGS.md` **A3**: the doc's Node 27 makes the exposition's most
ambitious genuinely-new claim but **without the technical grounding**. The condensed `ess-5`
keeps it anchored to the **BMS memory effect** (Bondi–van der Burg–Metzner; Strominger,
*Lectures on the Infrared Structure of Gravity and Gauge Theory*, 2017 — in the bibliography)
and explicitly frames it as "an entailment of the theory," not speculation. The decision was
to keep the prose uninterrupted by citation and let the physics carry the claim.

### What the full doc text adds that the condensed versions drop

- The **matter-has-memory / energy-merely-passes** framing and its refusal.
- The accumulation: "Radiation persists through decay… A wave can pass and still leave the world otherwise."
- The "**not to anthropomorphise energy**" guardrail.
- The naming of this as the exposition's **speculative horizon** and the sweep across all the works (radiation, transmission, lightning, projection, simulation, radio).

### Trade-off to weigh

The condensed pair is **more rigorous** (BMS-anchored, "not speculative"); the full doc text
is **broader and more programmatic** (it names the speculative horizon and ties the whole
oeuvre together). They are not strictly in conflict — the full text could sit as a framing
node with `ess-5` as its technical anchor. Note Node 30 (Oeuvre as Array, `ess-19`) already
performs some of this synthesising work.
